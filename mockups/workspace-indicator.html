Where am I, out of how many?

Three ways to keep the footer honest past six workspaces. All three keep today's look for small counts; they only differ in what happens when the row stops fitting.

The hard limit

172 pxusable footer width, after the gear and its balance spacer
26 pxper dot. 24 px is the smallest a hit target may be, so we cannot shrink our way out
10colours in the palette — colour stops being an identity at exactly 10 workspaces
6 dots fit. The 7th has nowhere to go.172 px, 6 × 26 px + gaps

Today the row is a horizontal scroller with hidden scrollbars. A mouse wheel scrolls vertically, so workspaces 7+ are only reachable from the switcher at the top — and nothing on screen says they exist.

Rolling page dots

Keeps the map
  • 1–6: unchanged. 7+: a five-dot window follows the active workspace.
  • A soft fade at the cut edge is the only hint that there is more — iOS home-screen page dots, in the rail.
  • Cost: dots stay unlabelled, so you can reliably jump to a neighbour but not to "the one for Billing"; the total is never visible.

Two-row grid

Everything in reach
  • Six per row, two rows: twelve workspaces stay one click away.
  • Cost: the footer doubles in height and eats the tab list; still unlabelled; breaks again at 13.
  • This is the option that looks like it scales but just moves the cliff.

Only two behaviours survive 50 workspaces: the pill and the rolling window. Only the pill also fixes identification, because it hands the choice to a list that already has names.

Recommended: position pill

  • Dots are a map. Past six the map has no room and the colours repeat — so stop pretending it is one.
  • No new screen: the pill opens the same workspace flyout that the switcher at the top already shows.
  • The footer answers one question — where am I — and defers which one to a labelled list.

What stays exactly as it is

  • The dot language at 1–6: colour, 9 px active, 15 px ring.
  • Settings gear, its balance spacer, footer height, 24 px hit targets.
  • Two-finger swipe between workspaces, and the labelled rows in the top switcher.
  • Nothing in storage or the provider seam.

First slice

Footer renders the same ItemsControl, but capped at six; a seventh workspace flips the row to the pill. The pill's dot calls the existing ShowWorkspaceFlyout, and the chevrons call the existing SelectRelativeSpace. Two properties on StudioViewModel, one Visibility pair, no migration.

Deferred on purpose: Ctrl+Alt+←/→ and Ctrl+1…9 to jump without the mouse, and archiving stale workspaces so the list stays short. Neither is needed to ship the fix.
